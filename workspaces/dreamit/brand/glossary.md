# DREAMit glossary

## Prefer
- **DREAMit** (exact casing)
- Fra hobby til webshop
- Webstudio / webshop-forløb
- Brandidentitet, logo, farver, lancering
- Menneskelig, premium, troværdig

## Avoid
- DreamIT / DREAMIT / Dreamit (wrong spellings)
- Generisk “digital agency” uden hobby→shop vinkel
- Cyan som bærende accent (explicitly fravalgt later)
- Gaming / neon as overall brand
- Heavy 3D everywhere (prefer one strong hero)

## Names
- **DREAMit** — public brand
- **FACCI** — internal OS only
