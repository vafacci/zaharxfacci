# DREAMit audience

## Primary (confirmed)
- Creative hobbyists and early entrepreneurs with a product idea but little branding/webshop experience.
- Example used in history: someone who crochets and wants to sell but doesn’t know naming, logo, colors, shop setup, or first marketing steps.
- Other **examples** (not exhaustive list): jewelry, ceramics, art, clothing, accessories.

## Needs / barriers
- Don’t know where to start.
- Missing coherent visual identity.
- Unsure how to plan/launch a shop.
- Need plain-language help, not agency jargon.

## Positioning note
- Sell the path from hobby → professional small online business — not only a technical site build.
