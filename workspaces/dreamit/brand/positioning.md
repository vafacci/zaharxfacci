# DREAMit positioning

**Brand spelling:** always **`DREAMit`** (DREAM + it). Not DreamIT / DREAMIT / Dreamit.  
**Source:** `_knowledge/DREAMit_Brand_Knowledge.md` (2026-10-15).

## What we are
- A webstudio / web-bureau-like project by Dayan and Frederik.
- Central promise: **“Fra hobby til webshop.”**
- Journey: hobby or product idea → brand identity → website/webshop → launch.
- More than “just a finished website” — help beginners build a small sellable presence.

## What we are not
- Not a generic enterprise agency.
- Not a gaming / neon tech demo brand.
- Not FACCI AGENT (FACCI is the OS; DREAMit is the client-facing brand).

## Differentiator
- Helps first-time makers who already create (e.g. crochet, jewelry, ceramics, art, apparel) but lack branding + shop know-how.
- Combines branding, digital design, and webshop help in one beginner-friendly path.
