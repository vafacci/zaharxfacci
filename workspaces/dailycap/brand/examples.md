# DailyCap draft copy examples

Style targets only — not published campaigns. Prefer clarity over hype.

## Product-core (safe framing)
1. How much can you spend today — without drowning in a full budget spreadsheet?
2. Your DailyCap is today’s room to spend. Tomorrow’s plan starts when today ends.
3. Saving should feel like progress, not punishment.

## Explored features (must mark proposed / not shipped)
1. Got money left today? We’re exploring Carry, Save, or Spread — not claiming it’s live.
2. Make discipline feel more like a game — noticeable rewards, never overwhelming noise.

## Avoid
- Guarantees of “safe” spend with zero risk.
- Fake before/after savings stats.
