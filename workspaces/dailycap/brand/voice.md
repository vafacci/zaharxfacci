# DailyCap brand voice

## Documented experience prefs (not a finished ToV manual)
- Simple and clear — answer the day’s spend question without overwhelm.
- Motivating / playful enough that saving feels rewarding — **not** guilt-heavy austerity.
- Gamification must be **noticeable** but **not overwhelming** (middle ground).
- Progress should feel real: getting better at the “game” should support better saving habits.

## Language
- No locked Danish-vs-English policy for public copy.
- Do not invent slang rules, emoji policy, or official brand mantras.

## Claims in copy
- Prefer capability language (“helps you see today’s room to spend”) over guarantees (“safely” / “never overspend”).
- Never invent UX that is only explored (Carry / Save / Spread) as shipped.
