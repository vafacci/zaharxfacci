# DailyCap claims policy

## Allowed
- Core question framing: how much can I spend today / daily spend room.
- Describe product as MVP-under-development unless owner proves launch.
- Discuss Carry / Save / Spread and gamification as **explored directions**.
- Honest limits: bank data delay, booked vs pending transactions.

## Forbidden without new evidence
- “Updates instantly on every purchase.”
- “Includes all card reservations / holds.”
- “Guarantees you won’t overspend.”
- “Already moves money to a real savings account.”
- “Already invests for you.”
- Carry/Save/Spread, streaks, or a specific reward system as **live features**.
- Documented average savings amounts or user counts.
- Invented visual identity (neon fintech, scandi premium, etc.).

## Provenance
- Label drafts `proposed`. Product claims need `observed` evidence.
