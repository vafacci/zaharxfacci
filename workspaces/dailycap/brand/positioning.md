# DailyCap positioning

**Sources:** `_knowledge/DailyCap_Brand_Knowledge.md` (2026-10-15).

## What we are
- Personal finance / budget app centered on: **“How much can I safely spend today?”**
- Daily disposable frame (**DailyCap**) instead of forcing users to reinterpret a huge budget every time.
- Aim: spend less and save more through better everyday decisions — motivating, not punishing.

## What we are not (on current evidence)
- Not an investment platform as the main product.
- Not a bank that holds or moves user money.
- Not a claim of real-time coverage of every card reservation.
- Not proof that Carry / Save / Spread, streaks, or investment allocation are already live.

## Differentiator (documented direction)
- Daily decision clarity: today’s room to spend.
- Explored: leftover daily money should matter (Carry / Save / Spread) — **explored, not shipped**.
- Gamification as engagement for discipline — balanced, not casino-loud and not invisible.

## Tech context (do not overclaim in marketing)
- Bank linking explored with **Nordea Danmark** via **Enable Banking**.
- AIS described as poll-based; pending/reserved transactions were a known hard problem.

## Open (do not invent)
- Launch date, pricing, exclusive market, logo, palette, official USP text.
