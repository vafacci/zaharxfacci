# DailyCap audience

## Primary need (qualitative — not a validated persona sheet)
- People who struggle to hold back spending and to save.
- Want a simple recurring daily decision — not overwhelming budget admin.
- Motivation / sense of progress matters for habit sticking.

## Geography / context
- Development examples use **DKK** and **Nordea Danmark**.
- Danish context is near — **not** proven as exclusive launch market.

## Unknown (do not invent)
- Age, income, gender, personas from interviews, willingness to pay.
