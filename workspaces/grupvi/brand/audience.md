# Grupvi audience

## Primary (confirmed)
- Students working in small project groups.
- Typical situation: members don’t necessarily know each other privately.

## Needs
- Fast access, shared chat, project overview.
- Low barrier to join (guest access without forced personal account is a product principle).

## Differentiation
- Study-specific gathering place with low onboarding friction.

## Open
- Exact age, geography, education type — not formally locked.
