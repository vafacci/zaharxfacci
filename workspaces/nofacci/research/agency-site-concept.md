# NOFACCI agency website — simple concept (pre-build)

**Status:** draft · **2026-10-15**  
**Owner note:** Keep it **basic**, inspired by Unique / Le Management pages — but for our AI creators, not “bliv model”-casting.

## What the site is for (plain language)

People who land on the page should:
1. Understand this is an **agency for virtual AI influencers**.
2. See the **four profiles** (name + short vibe + link placeholders).
3. Know how to **reach the agency** (simple contact — owner supplies details later).

That is the whole site for v1. No casting portal, no fake metrics, no huge marketing site.

## Inspiration → our basic page (observed → proposed)

| Inspiration pattern | We keep | We drop |
|---|---|---|
| Warm “you are in good hands” agency tone (Le) | Yes — calm, human | Their human-model care / family / ethics charter story |
| Clear sections + one next step (Unique) | Yes — simple structure | Open casting, age rules, photo upload |
| Trust → explain → CTA | Yes | “Send application as model” |

**Basic one-page (or few-section) outline — proposed:**
1. **Hero** — agency name + one short line (character before technology).
2. **How we work** — short: we manage distinct virtual creators; each has their own world.
3. **Talent / roster** — Theah Rose, Zuma Wild, Nova Reamon, Elion Lior (themes from brand pack only).
4. **Contact** — simple CTA (email/form later).
