# NOFACCI claims policy

## Allowed
- Describe confirmed profile names and high-level themes.
- Draft captions labeled **proposed** for human review.
- Note platform *as reported by owner* — never claim live verification.
- Character consistency and photoreal quality as creative goals.

## Allowed only with owner evidence
- Follower counts, engagement metrics, revenue, Fanvue traffic.
- Exact ages, cities, full bios.
- Live Instagram/TikTok/X account availability after bans/suspensions.
- Merch SKUs, shop URLs still active under which name.

## Forbidden
- Invent testimonials, quotes, or “fans say…”.
- Pretend all profiles share one voice or one audience.
- Call Zuma a surfer.
- Apply Linnea store/merch facts as current Theah facts without confirmation.
- Claim Instagram/TikTok status beyond what owner reported.
- Auto-publish or claim published posts from FACCI drafts.
