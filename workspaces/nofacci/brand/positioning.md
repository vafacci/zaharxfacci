# NOFACCI positioning

**Sources:** `_knowledge/NOFACCI_Brand_Knowledge.md` (2026-10-15).

## What we are
- Management / content activity around **virtual AI influencers**.
- Portfolio of distinct creator characters under one agency layer.
- Principle: **character before technology** — face, personality, interests, visual world first.

## What we are not
- Not FACCI AGENT (FACCI is the OS).
- Not VAFACCI (music/artist identity — separate).
- Not one generic “AI brand” voice for all profiles.
- Not proof of fan counts, revenue, or live account health without evidence.
