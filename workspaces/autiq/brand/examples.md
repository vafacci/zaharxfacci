# AUTIQ approved example captions

Owner-curated style targets. Prefer this craft over generic AI marketing.

## Instagram
1. Tired of rewriting the same caption three ways? Draft once, review, then decide — nothing posts without you.
2. Content Autopilot is for operators who want fewer blank pages, not autopilot without brakes.
3. One weekly theme → Instagram / LinkedIn / Facebook drafts. You keep the final say.

## LinkedIn
1. Most “AI content tools” optimize for volume. We optimize for draft → approval → publish control.
2. If your bottleneck is repetitive channel variants, automate the drafting — keep humans on taste and claims.
3. AUTIQ Content Autopilot: structured weekly drafts, brand voice constraints, explicit publish gate.

## Facebook
1. Practical update: we help turn one idea into channel drafts you can approve before anything goes live.
2. No fake success stories. Just clearer drafts and a review step you control.
3. Want less copy-paste between channels? Start with a weekly theme and review the drafts.

## Anti-examples (do not write like this)
- “Revolutionary AI that will 10x your revenue overnight!!!!”
- “Our clients gained 50k followers in a week” (unless observed evidence exists).
- Emoji spam + five hashtags + vague futurism.
