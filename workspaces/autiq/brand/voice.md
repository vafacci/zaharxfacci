# AUTIQ brand voice

## Tone
- Clear, modern, trustworthy, precise.
- Professional without corporate fluff.
- Confident and calm — never hype, never “revolutionary”.
- Short sentences. One idea per caption.

## Visual signal (confirmed logo brief — not a full ToV guide)
- Desired feel: **premium tech / automation**.
- Wordmark: minimal, geometric sans (Geist-like), high contrast.
- Logo canvas: background `#070809`, text `#F4F5F6`.
- Logo brief fravalg: no icon, glow, gradients, shadows, orange, decorations (logo scope — not a total ban on all future media).

## Language
- Danish or English depending on channel (no exclusive language rule locked in sources).
- Instagram: conversational, concrete.
- LinkedIn: operator/business tone, outcome-focused.
- Facebook: plain language, practical.

## Claims
- Product claims must be evidence-backed or phrased as capability, not proof.
- Do NOT invent customers, testimonials, quotes, metrics, hours saved, or case studies.
- Do NOT invent partners, logos, or “as seen in” lines.

## Caption craft
- Prefer one concrete CTA (review a draft, book a call, reply, save for later).
- Max two hashtags. No emoji spam (at most one, optional).
- Status stays draft until a human publishes.

## Brand guidelines
- **AUTIQ** — product/concept for **content creators**, with **content automation** as the explicit focus (confirmed in brand history).
- In FACCI ops: first concrete offer is **Content Autopilot** — draft → human review → schedule, with approval gates.
- AI is **infrastructure**, not the product hero.

## Differentiator (FACCI-confirmed for drafts)
- Human stays in control of publish and high-risk actions.
- Concrete ops outcomes (less repetitive drafting), not hype metrics.

## Offer framing (safe for drafts)
- Help creators turn ideas into channel-ready drafts faster.
- Keep brand voice and approvals intact.
- Do not invent features, prices, customers, or launch status not in sources.

## Open (do not invent)
- Full mission statement, pricing, exact feature list, competitor USP matrix.
