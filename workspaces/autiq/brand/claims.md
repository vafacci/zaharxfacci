# AUTIQ claims policy

## Allowed (capability / process)
- We help draft captions and calendar ideas for human review.
- Humans approve before publish.
- Content automation / Content Autopilot framing as draft workflow.
- Drafts stay draft until a human publishes.
- Visual identity intent: premium tech / automation (from logo brief).

## Allowed only with owner-provided evidence
- Specific customer names, logos, case studies.
- Exact hours saved, revenue, reach, conversion rates.
- Partnerships, “as seen in”, awards.
- Live product feature lists beyond confirmed framing.

## Forbidden (never invent)
- Fake testimonials, quotes, or “clients say…”.
- Invented metrics, rankings, or #1 claims.
- Guarantees of virality, growth, or revenue.
- Hype: revolutionary, pioneering, game-changer, unveiling the future.
- Claiming Instagram/TikTok handles, follower counts, or published video results without evidence.
- Treating FACCI AGENT as the public product name for AUTIQ ads.

## Provenance labels
- `observed` — from brand pack, vault, or owner input.
- `inferred` — reasonable reading of observed facts (label it).
- `proposed` — draft suggestion for owner review.
- `blocked` — missing evidence or high-risk external action.
