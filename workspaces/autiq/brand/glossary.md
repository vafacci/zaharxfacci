# AUTIQ glossary

## Prefer
- AUTIQ (exact casing in brand/logo contexts)
- Content Autopilot / content automation
- Draft → review → approve → publish
- Human approval / owner gate
- Brand pack / brand voice
- Content creators / creator ops
- Premium tech / automation (visual intent)

## Avoid
- Autopilot (alone, without “approval” context)
- Revolutionary / pioneering / game-changer
- “Set and forget”
- Guaranteed growth / viral
- Fake social proof language
- Orange / glow / logo decorations (per logo brief)

## Product names
- **AUTIQ** — the business brand
- **Content Autopilot** — first FACCI-framed offer
- **FACCI** — internal AI-org OS (usually not the public hero of AUTIQ ads)
- **Image Studio** — separate image tool; not the content loop itself

## Visual tokens (logo brief — observed)
- Background `#070809`, text `#F4F5F6`
- Geometric sans similar to Geist Sans, medium, slightly wide tracking
